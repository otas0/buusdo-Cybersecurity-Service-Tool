Notice on suspending the China mainland entry pages and region auto-guide

Dear users,

Thank you for your interest in buusdo Cybersecurity Service Tool (the “Project”). This notice explains the suspension of the China mainland entry pages and the automatic guide to the product site.

1. Reason and scope of suspension

Full checking runs on Cloudflare Workers ( https://buusdocst.mtsu.workers.dev ). In some mainland China networks that host can be slow, time out, or unreachable, so checks may fail.

To avoid confusion when full checking is unstable, from 22 November 2026 the Project suspends regular service of the China mainland entry pages (GitHub Pages: https://otas0.github.io/buusdocst/ ). Product explainers, privacy summaries, and install guides formerly on this site are removed; only this notice remains. The full product UI lives on the Workers site.

Scope is limited to these entry pages; it does not mean the Workers product site is unavailable on every network worldwide.

2. Region / network auto-guide (switch to the other site)

There are two related entry points:

  • China mainland entry — https://otas0.github.io/buusdocst/ (this notice; shown when Workers is unreachable)
  • Workers full checker — https://buusdocst.mtsu.workers.dev (product UI; e.g. /zh-Hans, /zh-Hant, /en)

On any China entry page, the site probes whether Workers is reachable (read-only health check; see §3):

  1. Workers reachable : auto-guide to the matching Workers locale path (e.g. /zh-Hans, /zh-Hant, /en).
  2. Workers unreachable : stay on this notice; no availability promise for mainland access.

“Auto-guide” means China entry → Workers checker for your language — not a set of country-specific product mirrors.

3. Network probe and privacy

  • Probe : browser GET /api/health to Workers (short timeout, no cookies) to decide reachability and guide.
  • Not used for : profiling, ads, marketing, or other purposes.
  • Does not call : /api/check (that runs only after you open Workers and submit an address).
  • Language preference : 中文 / EN choice stays in local localStorage (buusdocst-china-lang) only — not uploaded.

Full privacy policy: https://buusdocst.mtsu.workers.dev/en/privacy

4. Restoration

Restoration of the China entry is not scheduled. When conditions allow, a new notice will be published. Until then the Project makes no promise about mainland reachability of the entry pages or Workers.

5. Contact

If you are on a Cloudflare-reachable network but cannot open Workers, or have questions about this notice, contact us via GitHub Issues. We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your understanding.

buusdo Cybersecurity Service Tool developers
