Notice on the China mainland entry and Workers force-redirect

Dear users,

Thank you for your interest in buusdo Cybersecurity Service Tool (the “Project”). This notice explains how mainland China networks are guided between Workers and this page.

1. Reason and scope

Full checking runs on Cloudflare Workers ( https://buusdocst.mtsu.workers.dev ). In some mainland China networks that host can be slow, time out, or unreachable, so checks may fail.

From 22 November 2026 the China mainland GitHub Pages entry ( https://otas0.github.io/buusdocst/ ) keeps only this notice (explainers / privacy summaries / install guides are offline). From 23 November 2026, when Cloudflare marks a visitor as mainland China ( CF-IPCountry=CN ) on the Workers product site, the Project force-redirects them to this notice so an unstable network is not mistaken for a working full checker. The full product UI still lives on Workers; /api/* is never redirected (extensions and machine clients keep working).

Scope targets mainland China network conditions; it does not mean Workers is unavailable on every network worldwide.

2. How the two entries work together

There are two related entry points:

  • China mainland entry — https://otas0.github.io/buusdocst/ (this notice; mainland visitors are force-sent here from Workers)
  • Workers full checker — https://buusdocst.mtsu.workers.dev (product UI; e.g. /zh-Hans, /zh-Hant, /en)
  1. Open Workers from mainland China : force-redirect to this notice (ops opt-out: ?china_workers_stay=1).
  2. Stay on this notice : read-only health probe shows reachability only — no auto-bounce back to Workers (avoids a redirect loop). Open Workers manually when you need the full checker.
  3. Workers unreachable : stay on this notice; no availability promise for mainland access.

3. Network detection and privacy

  • On Workers : Cloudflare CF-IPCountry (only to decide the force-redirect — not for profiling or ads).
  • On this page : browser GET /api/health to Workers (short timeout, no cookies) to show reachability — no auto-redirect.
  • Does not call : /api/check (that runs only after you open Workers and submit an address).
  • Language preference : 中文 / EN choice stays in local localStorage (buusdocst-china-lang) only — not uploaded.

Full privacy policy: https://buusdocst.mtsu.workers.dev/en/privacy (mainland visitors may be guided back here when opening that link).

4. Restoration

Restoration of the China entry is not scheduled. When conditions allow, a new notice will be published. Until then the Project makes no promise about mainland reachability of the entry pages or Workers.

5. Contact

If you are on a Cloudflare-reachable network but cannot open Workers, or have questions about this notice, contact us via GitHub Issues. We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your understanding.
